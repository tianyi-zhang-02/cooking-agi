The third mistake in [my job-search notes](journey.en.md) was “not understanding the roles”. Its direct consequence: I thought I was preparing for “a job search”, when I was really preparing for several unrelated exams at once.

## If you prepare for several roles at once, how much transfers?

Select the roles you plan to apply for together, and see how many topic areas that means and how many of them are shared.

<!-- widget:roles -->

Select all five, then keep just one, and compare the bar. That is the real cost of applying to everything: not the time spent sending résumés, but that **your preparation cannot compound**.

## Roughly what each role tests

| Role | Main areas |
| --- | --- |
| Data Engineer | SQL, data modeling, ETL and pipelines, distributed-systems basics, data structures and algorithms |
| Data Scientist | statistical inference, A/B testing, product sense, SQL, probability, ML fundamentals |
| MLE | ML fundamentals, deep learning and LLMs, ML system design, data structures and algorithms, probability, linear algebra |
| SDE | data structures and algorithms, system design, object-oriented design and engineering practice, distributed-systems basics |
| Quant Researcher | probability, expectation and mental math, logic puzzles, statistical inference, linear algebra |
