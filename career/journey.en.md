## Timeline

- **Around August 2025**: started preparing for the job search.
- **After that**: started applying at scale. Small, mid-size and big tech; Data Engineer, Data Scientist, MLE, SDE. I applied to all of them.
- **Around the same time**: I was also fortunate to interview for Quant Researcher roles at HRT, SIG and Tower Research.

## Three mistakes I made

1. **No clear through-line.**
2. **No consistent interview performance.** To be honest, I still do not have it today.
3. **Not understanding the roles.**

All of this is normal. There is a real gap between school and industry :) Stepping into the real world looks like this.

## Phase one: applying to everything

At the start I knew nothing. I did not filter by company size or by role; I applied wherever I could.

This is where it went wrong: **with limited time, every interview was different**, because they were not even the same domain. Data Engineer, Data Scientist, MLE, SDE and Quant Researcher test almost non-overlapping things, so I could not prepare properly, let alone get deep in any one direction.
