# Company marks

These local assets identify companies in the community directory. They do not represent partnerships, endorsements, or reader career outcomes. Preset metadata, aliases, asset sources, and available brand guidelines are tracked in `site/company-icons.json` at the repository root.

- Simple Icons 16.33.0: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
- SVG Logos, distributed through `@iconify-json/logos` 1.2.15: https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos
- HRT and ServiceNow: SVGs from their official websites, with exact source URLs in the registry.

Simple Icons and SVG Logos are distributed under CC0, but this does not grant trademark rights or supersede individual brand restrictions. Names and marks belong to their respective owners. See each entry's source and any supplied guidelines or license metadata before updating a mark.

Assets keep their original geometry. The ServiceNow symbol is extracted from the green symbol path in its official header wordmark so it remains legible at icon size. Display uses a consistent monochrome treatment and contain-fit sizing; no tracking service or remote logo API is called by the page. Missing marks use the full company name, not initials or invented logos.

To add or correct a mark, submit the company name, official website, and public logo resources through `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/company-icon.yml`. A maintainer reviews the source and SVG before adding it; submitted URLs are never automatically fetched or embedded.
